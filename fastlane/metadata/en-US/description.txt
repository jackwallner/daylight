Daylight counts the minutes you spend outside and shows them climbing toward a target you set. Then it does the part nobody else does: it works out the latest you could head out and still reach that target before the sun goes down.

Twenty minutes means something different at noon than it does twenty minutes before sunset. That is the whole idea.

TODAY
- Minutes you have spent in daylight, read from Apple Health, as the lead number
- Your progress toward the daily target you chose
- Sunrise, sunset, and today's day length
- A bar showing how much of the daylight window has already passed

YOUR DEADLINE
- Short of your target? The app works out the latest you could start and still reach it before the light goes
- The deadline is anchored on sunset, so it moves with the season instead of the clock
- When the target is genuinely out of reach, it says so plainly and tells you what tomorrow offers

TRENDS
- Daily minutes, days on target, and your current streak
- An unfinished day never breaks a live streak
- Compare today's available daylight with the same day a month ago, a number that comes from the sun whether or not you went outside

ON YOUR WRIST AND HOME SCREEN
- Apple Watch app leading with your minutes and your target
- Watch complications in circular, rectangular, inline, and corner styles, each showing progress toward the target
- Home screen and Lock Screen widgets

CALCULATED ON YOUR DEVICE
Sunrise and sunset come from the NOAA solar position algorithm and one approximate location fix. There is no server, no lookup service, and no background tracking. Polar day and polar night are handled properly, including the civil twilight window when the sun never rises.

APPLE HEALTH
The app reads Time in Daylight and never writes to Apple Health. You choose which sources count. A phone and a watch from the same app are collapsed into one row so nothing is counted twice.

A NOTE ON WHAT THIS IS
Daylight reports what your devices recorded and what the sun offers. Your daily target is your own preference, not a recommendation. The app does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent anything, and makes no claim about mood, sleep, vitamin D, or eyesight.

Time in Daylight is recorded by Apple Watch. Without one, the minutes figure stays at zero and the app says so on the first screen; everything computed from the sun still works.
